The issue I want to focus on is how air pollutants and other weather factors can worsen pollen allergies by affecting the pollen grain count. Specifically, I want to examine different factors like temperature, precipitation, wind and air pollutants like PM2.5, PM10, nitrogen dioxide, ozone, carbon monoxide, and sulfur dioxide. This issue is important as people all around the world suffer from pollen allergies, which can worsen respiratory issues such as asthma. In addition, air pollution is becoming more prominent as human society expands to nearly all areas of the Earth. As such, a model or identified relationship between pollen grain fluctuation and gaseous air pollution is necessary.
